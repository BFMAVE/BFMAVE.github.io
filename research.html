Papers and publications

Research

My economics work includes forecasting, decision-making under uncertainty, public investment and ergodicity economics. I also work on AI evaluation and quantitative methods. My mathematical research centres on Markov chains and stochastic matrices, alongside work on group rings and braces and recent explorations of AI-assisted research in Ramsey theory.

More recently, my interests have turned to the economic impact of AI, particularly its effects on scientific discovery and innovation.

I have also served as an examiner, supervisor or co-supervisor for master’s theses spanning a wide range of economics and business topics.
