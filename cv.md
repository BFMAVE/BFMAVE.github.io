## Profile

I am a quantitative economist (PhD) and mathematician (MSc) by training, and I think of myself as a data scientist. My interests centre on economics and AI. More recently, I have become particularly interested in the economic impact of AI and its role in scientific discovery and innovation. My research spans economic forecasting, model evaluation and AI in mathematics and science.

## Selected projects

- **AI and scientific discovery.** Interested in how AI changes the pace and direction of knowledge production, the division of work between researchers and AI, and verification costs.

- **AI-supported science for policy.** Through a VUB collaboration with the JRC, I was involved in developing evaluation templates and contributed to a validation protocol for AI-supported research outputs.

- **FARI / AI4Gov-X.** Co-authored an internal review of AI4Scale requirements and prototype validation, covering feasibility, prioritisation, deployment, governance and process ownership.

- **Brussels Metro 3.** Led scenario-based public-transport accessibility research using official transport and open-map data.

- **Institutional AI adoption.** Technical implementation lead for LLM-assisted classification of an initial 4,684 course information sheets, combining human validation and quality controls.

- **Mathematics and AI-assisted discovery.** Research on stochastic matrices and spectral regions; an illustrated [Karpelevič project](https://bfmave.github.io/karpelevic/)  and a documented case study of AI-assisted proof development.
